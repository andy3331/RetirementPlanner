How this plan is modeled

Brokerage is the bridge from retirement to 401k unlock. The 401k compounds with its own assumptions, stops receiving contributions at retirement, stays untouched until unlock, then funds withdrawals first. If brokerage still has a balance after unlock, it acts as reserve capital. Social Security reduces the withdrawal need starting at your claim age.
