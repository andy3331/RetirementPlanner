How this plan is modeled

Brokerage is the bridge from retirement to 401k unlock. The 401k compounds with its own assumptions, stops receiving contributions at retirement, stays untouched until unlock, then funds withdrawals first. If brokerage still has a balance after unlock, it acts as reserve capital. Social Security reduces the withdrawal need starting at your claim age.

Healthcare & ACA (advanced)
ACA household size appears when you switch to income-sensitive mode.

Manual mode adds the pre-Medicare healthcare cost, scaled by the ACA stress setting, on top of retirement spending. Income-sensitive mode estimates a premium from a benchmark plus a subsidy rule, using an income proxy from wages before retirement and then 401k withdrawals, realized gains, and Social Security after. Presets: current = 100%, reduced = 150%, severe = 200%. Policy-sensitive values live in policy-config.js.
